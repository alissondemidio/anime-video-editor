# Media and copyright policy

This software edits media supplied by the user. It does not automatically download anime episodes or bypass DRM.

Require each media asset to record provenance, ownership or license, and permitted usage. Commentary, critique or transformation may affect copyright analysis, but do not guarantee permission or platform acceptance. Offer replacement/flagging for questionable assets.

Demo media must be original or permissively licensed. The application should not embed proprietary anime names, logos or clips in shipped samples.

All processing is local by default. Remote AI providers must be opt-in and clearly disclose data transfer.
